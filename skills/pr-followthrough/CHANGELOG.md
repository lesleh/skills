# Changes to pr-followthrough

Each entry lists what changed in how the skill behaves, newest first. The `metadata.version` field in [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) holds the date of the newest entry, so you can match your installed copy to an entry here.

## 2026-11-18

- The skill resolves a review thread only after you approve its reply and the reply is posted.
- The next round skips a thread whose reply is waiting for your approval.
- The skill can finish with replies still waiting for your approval. Its report lists them in full.
